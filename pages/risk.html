Risk disclosure

Leveraged products

Spread bets and CFDs are complex instruments and come with a high risk of losing money rapidly due to leverage. You should consider whether you understand how they work and whether you can afford to take the high risk of losing your money. Losses can exceed the amount you expected to risk, for example when prices gap past a stop.

Hypothetical results

Backtested, simulated and paper-trading results are hypothetical. They are produced with the benefit of hindsight, cannot fully reflect real trading costs, fills, liquidity or your own decisions, and do not guarantee future performance. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results.

Automated trading

Cerebro carries out rules that you configure and approve. Software, data feeds, broker systems and internet connections can fail or be delayed, and automated trading can open or close positions you would not have chosen at that moment. You remain responsible for your account, your settings and your trades.

No advice

Cerebro is software. It does not provide personalised investment advice, does not assess whether a strategy is suitable for you, and does not manage money on your behalf.

Your broker

Trades are placed on your own account with your broker under their terms. Cerebro is not a broker and does not hold client money.
