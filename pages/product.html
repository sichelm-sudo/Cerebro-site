Product

One platform for the whole life of a trading strategy.

Below is what Cerebro does, area by area. Each feature is marked In beta if it works in the platform today, or Coming soon if it is still being built.

Strategy development

From an idea to exact, testable rules.

In beta

Building blocks

A library of tested blocks - indicators checked against an industry-standard reference, candle and chart patterns, sessions, trend and range filters - combined into strict rules with every setting declared.

Coming soon

AI Strategy Builder

Describe an approach in plain English and get rules back, with every interpretation labelled and variations to compare.

In beta

Frozen versions

A strategy version can't be edited after testing - changes make a new version, so results always match the rules that produced them.

Backtesting

Results you can trust, after costs.

In beta

One engine

The same code decides in backtests and live trading, and never looks at future prices - checked automatically for every building block.

In beta

Realistic costs

Spreads (wider late in the UK evening) and stop slippage are built into every result.

In beta

Normal ranges

Thousands of reshuffled trade histories show what good and bad runs normally look like for each strategy.

Coming soon

Robustness reports

Parameter sensitivity and stress tests summarised for each strategy.

Automation

Your rules, carried out consistently.

In beta

Paper and demo trading

Runs strategies on live prices in paper trading and on IG demo accounts, sizing each trade to your risk.

In beta

Trade management

Stops and targets on every order, profit locks to breakeven and beyond, trailing stops and time limits - applied as tested.

In beta

Broker reconciliation

Positions are checked against the broker every round, so nothing is lost or invented across a restart.

Coming soon

Live accounts and modes

Live accounts, and a choice of fully automated, semi-automated (you approve) or manual tier moves.
