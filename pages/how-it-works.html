How it works

Every strategy has to earn its next step.

Cerebro moves strategies through five stages. Each one asks a harder question than the last, and a strategy that stops answering it steps back down.

01 · Lab

Coming soon

Does the idea hold up on history?

Exact rules, years of minute data, realistic costs, and a judgement on years the strategy was never built on. A strategy needs enough trades and a healthy profit factor after costs to move on.

02 · Sandbox

In beta

Does it hold up on today's prices?

Paper trading on live prices under your risk settings - with a second copy running without the market filter, so the filter's effect is measured, not assumed.

03 · Bench

In beta

Does it hold up with real fills?

Trading on a broker demo account. Results are placed against the strategy's normal range to show whether it is behaving as testing said it would.

04 · Live

Coming soon

Do you want to run it?

You decide what goes live on your own account. Cerebro carries out your rules within your limits.

05 · Portfolio

In beta

Does it still belong?

Strategy health, correlation and currency exposure across everything you run. Strategies outside their normal range are flagged to step down; strong ones are put forward to step up.

The rules behind the moves

Set by testing, not by feel.

The rules for promoting and demoting strategies are being set by simulating thousands of random portfolios on data they have never seen - so the rules aren't fitted to history after the fact. In development

You choose how much happens automatically: Cerebro can move strategies by its rules, ask you to approve each move, or leave every decision to you. Coming soon
